We are currently seeking a high calibre professional to join our team as a Actuarial Assistant.
In this role you will:
Carry out the end-to-end actuarial reporting process, including data preparation, assumption setting, model configuration, model execution, results review, analysis and submission.
Produce monthly, quarterly and annual reporting for insurance entities under IFRS 17.
Prepare management information under other reporting bases, such as embedded value.
Ensure that financial reporting activities comply with internal controls and internal and external audit requirements.
Maintain robust control structures, governance arrangements and documentation standards.
Support analytical reviews for new business management, sensitivities, analysis of change, business projections and the impact of assumption changes.
Support assessments relating to VFA eligibility and onerous contracts under IFRS 17.
To be successful you will need:
A university degree in a quantitative subject, together with progress towards or completion of professional actuarial qualifications.
Good spoken and written English.
Strong communication skills.
Good interpersonal and relationship-building skills.
A structured and detail-oriented approach to analytical and reporting work.
The ability to work collaboratively with colleagues and stakeholders across the business.

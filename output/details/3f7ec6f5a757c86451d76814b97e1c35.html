Captured job description

We're hiring for a fast-growing insurtech startup that's rebuilding how insurers design, run, and govern their actuarial and financial models.
If you're an actuary who's tired of legacy systems and wants to build the tools insurers will use for the next decade, this is your seat.
What you'll do
Design, build, and test actuarial models that power real decisions on forecasting, risk, pricing, and capital
Dig into complex data sets and turn them into insight, not just output
Work closely with cross-functional teams (product, tech, actuarial) and Big 4 actuarial consultants
Keep models accurate, efficient, and audit-ready against evolving regulatory frameworks
What you'll bring
5+ years of actuarial experience, ideally with exposure to IFRS 17, RBC 2, or Solvency II
Hands-on experience with actuarial modelling and coding tools/software (Prophet, Python, SQL, VBA etc.)
Sharp problem-solving skills and the ability to explain complex things simply
Fluency in Mandarin Chinese or Korean is a strong advantage, given regional stakeholder coverage
Who thrives here
This is a startup, not a legacy insurer. The actuary who does well is comfortable with ambiguity, likes building things from scratch, and would rather shape a process than inherit one. You're curious about technology, take ownership, and you're energised by moving fast.

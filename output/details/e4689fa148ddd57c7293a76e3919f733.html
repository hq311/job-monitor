Job Description
Responsible for performing sensitivity analysis on reserves and solvency.
Responsible for performing MAS Stress test and ORSA reporting.
Responsible for performing the protected liabilities required for Policyholders Protection Fund (PPF) reporting.
Work closely with modelling team on enhancements and implementation.
Maintain proper documentation and controls in areas of responsibility.
Review existing work processes and identify opportunities to streamline, enhance, automate, or eliminate redundant activities to improve efficiency, control effectiveness, and overall productivity.
Assist in audit request, ad-hoc actuarial investigations and corporate initiatives from time to time.
Assist in preparing materials for the various Committees and Board meetings.
Job Requirements
Minimum Degree certification in Actuarial Science or any related field.
Minimum 4 - 6 years of working experience in actuarial field.
Strong mathematical and analytical skills
Good communication skills, both written and spoken
Able to work independently, self-motived and out-come oriented
Meticulous with a keen eye for detail
Team player with good interpersonal skills and positive attitude
Proficient in Microsoft Office suite (Excel, Word, PPT, Access)
Intermediate to advance knowledge of actuarial projection software, i.e., Prophet, DCS.

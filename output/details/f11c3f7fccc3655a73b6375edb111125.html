Captured job description

As a Strategic Broker Relationship Manager, you will develop a strategic trading relationship with our top tier and strategic brokers with a focus on establishing & maintaining strong broker relationships, developing sound business development strategies, managing the sales process & pipeline and capturing detailed insights for future stratgeic direction.
The role requires driving accountability within the Underwriting community Commercial, driving and owning the trading relationship, whilst fostering a multi-tier relationship across all levels in both organisations and all lines of business.
Key responsibilities
Working with the Head of CDM,APAC and the Head of CI APAC, setting the strategic direction with our top tier and strategic brokers, ensuring alignment across APAC and agreeing and monitoring key growth initiatives.
Managing our top tier brokers and developing a holistic understanding of the brokers’ businesses to identify both strategic & transactional opportunities for Zurich.
Working closely with underwriting, claims and risk engineering (ZRS) to communicate broker strategy whilst bringing the best of Zurich to the market.
Working with the Head of CDM,APAC, monitoring portfolio and share of wallet (SOW) performance overall and per line with each broker, setting clear improvement targets YOY.
Translate and articulate broker performance data in to the business to ensure appropriate actions and output based on the information presented.
Gaining, capturing and analysing competitor intelligence data and developing plans to gain competitive advantage, identifying growth potential in each designated broker.
Understanding Zurich’s value proposition across all lines and segments and promoting this with the broker to strengthen relationships, leading to profitable business growth.
Designing an infrastructure for a multi-tiered relationship and embedding this across appropriate lines and segments
Ensuring regular and consistent communication between Zurich and the broker to improve the relationship and effectiveness across both organisations and to resolve service or relationship issues
Working closely with the APAC regional team, manage consistent messages and delivery of Zurich propositions.
Working collaboratively with the U/W’s and LOB Heads to be a focal point of contact to the market in respect of Multi-line and complex deals.
Assist the Underwriting teams in navigating brokers to manage and negotiate major deals, assisting in increasing Zurich’s product density and cross sell per client whilst identifying opportunities for business improvement.
Acting as a conduit between each broking partner and Zurich across all levels to improve communication and effective working relationships.
Bringing together the best of Zurich in articulating and delivering our Value Proposition to the market.
Develop and maintain relationships with key brokers in support of business retention and new business acquisition.
Representing Zurich at conferences and functions and ensuring other relevant Zurich Stakeholders are included
Support the Head of CDM, APAC, in the production of Broker Exec packs, Exec meeting, monthly reporting and negotiation and monitoring of Strategic Carrier Agreements

We are currently seeking a high calibre professional to join our team as Associate.
In this role you will:
The role sits within the Par & Universal Life Fund Management and Experience Studies team.
Primarily responsible for supporting experience monitoring, setting actuarial assumptions, performing impact and sensitivity testing, and contributing to the annual Par bonus recommendation and implementation.
Supports the monthly and quarterly production of management information for internal stakeholders.
Contributes to ad hoc actuarial initiatives and projects to enhance existing processes.
Works closely with internal and external stakeholders to deliver assigned outputs on time and to the required quality.
Maintains documentation in line with HSBC standards and guidelines.
To be successful you will need:
Relevant Life Actuarial experience, candidate with less experience / non-actuarial experience will be considered for Associate position.
Good communication skills, both written and spoken.
Ability to work independently, interact with other departments
Good technical, analytical and problem solving skills.
Team player with good interpersonal skills and positive attitude.
Experience in SQL/Python and/or Prophet is a plus.
You’ll achieve more at HSBC.

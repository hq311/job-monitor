About the company
A prominent global reinsurer with an agile regional presence, dedicated to fostering technical excellence and driving accelerated professional growth across Asia.
About the job
This high-visibility leadership position offers complete strategic ownership of regional actuarial pricing frameworks while driving commercial strategy and guiding high-performing technical teams.
Spearhead regional pricing evaluations, experience investigations, and treaty structuring to ensure profitable portfolio growth and optimal risk selection.
Elevate team performance and delivery standards by providing technical mentorship, quality oversight, and peer reviews for a team of pricing professionals.
Partner with underwriting and client management functions to construct innovative, risk-adjusted reinsurance solutions that strengthen client relationships.
Optimize pricing frameworks, cash flow models, and assumption setting processes to maintain robust technical governance across evolving market conditions.
About the manager/team
You will join a high-autonomy, meritocratic leadership team that values technical innovation, cross-functional collaboration, and streamlined decision-making processes.
Skills and experience required
Fellowship or full actuarial qualification from a recognized global professional body (e.g., IFoA, SOA, FIAA) alongside proficiency in analytical tools like SAS or SQL.
Deep functional expertise in Life and Health actuarial pricing, cash flow modeling, and experience analysis.
Demonstrated sector experience within reinsurance or a direct life insurer with comprehensive technical exposure to regional deal evaluations.
Proven track record of guiding project workstreams, reviewing complex actuarial deliverables, and mentoring junior team members.
Advanced commercial acumen with the ability to translate complex technical risks into clear strategic insights for diverse stakeholders.

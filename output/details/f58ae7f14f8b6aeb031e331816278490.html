Captured job description

Responsibilities:
Price participating, non-participating, and investment-linked products, aligning profitability with business strategy.
Support the execution of the overall product and pricing strategy for life insurance offerings.
Develop pricing methodologies based on actuarial principles and regulatory standards.
Ensure compliance with internal policies and external regulatory requirements for product launches.
Collaborate with internal departments to integrate new individual life product features and initiate process improvements.
Lead discussions with reinsurers to establish effective reinsurance structures when necessary.
Manage accountability regarding business and regulatory compliance risks, implementing appropriate risk mitigation strategies.
Stay informed about industry trends and emerging technologies that could impact risk management.
Requirements:
Qualified or nearly qualified Actuary from a recognized actuarial professional body.
Minimum of 5 years of experience in the actuarial field.
Detail-oriented, efficient, self-motivated, and capable of working under pressure.
Strong interpersonal, writing, and verbal communication skills.
Proficient in technical and analytical skills.

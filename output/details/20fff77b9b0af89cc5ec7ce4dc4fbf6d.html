About the company
A premier international financial institution with a strong Singapore footprint, committed to continuous business innovation, robust risk management, and long-term professional development.
About the job
This strategic actuarial audit role offers broad operational visibility and direct executive engagement while accelerating your trajectory toward team management.
Drive comprehensive technical evaluations of reserving frameworks, pricing strategies, capital models, and IFRS 17 compliance to enhance enterprise governance.
Deliver high-impact risk assessments and actionable insights directly to the Chief Actuary, Chief Risk Officer, and Audit Committee.
Champion independent reviews of actuarial methodologies across diverse business units to safeguard organizational integrity.
Collaborate with cross-functional leaders to refine modeling standards and optimize financial reporting workflows.
Execute structured leadership responsibilities designed to prepare you for direct line management in your next career phase.
About the manager/team
You will join a dynamic group of analytical specialists under the guidance of a seasoned actuarial leader who offers dedicated mentorship to elevate your commercial acumen and executive presence.
Skills and experience required
Proven experience within actuarial or risk management domains, with technical exposure across valuation, reserving, pricing, or IFRS 17 reporting.
Fellowship or near-fellowship credentials from a recognized professional body including FSA, FIA, FIAA, or equivalent.
Advanced capability with actuarial modeling platforms such as Prophet or AXIS alongside standard regulatory accounting frameworks.
Strong desire to step into an analytical oversight role with clear ambitions to lead and mentor team members.
Outstanding communication skills with the ability to translate complex technical findings into clear advice for senior executives.

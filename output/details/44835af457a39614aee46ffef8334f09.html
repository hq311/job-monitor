about the company
A Tier-1 financial advisory practice with a renowned global footprint, dedicated to delivering market-leading risk solutions while providing continuous long-term career development for its professionals.
about the job
This pivotal actuarial advisory role offers direct strategic impact, empowering you to own high-value client solutions and accelerate your trajectory toward practice leadership.
Spearhead sophisticated non-life consulting engagements including capital modeling, pricing strategies, climate risk evaluation, and M&A due diligence.
Transform complex quantitative modeling outputs into strategic executive insights to guide C-suite business decisions.
Drive practice growth and market expansion through innovative service offerings and impactful thought leadership.
Optimize consulting methodologies by embedding advanced data science tools and technical frameworks into core solutions.
Lead project execution streams with end-to-end accountability while ensuring exceptional technical quality and delivery standards.
about the manager/team
You will work alongside vision-driven directors and partners within a highly collaborative setting that offers direct leadership sponsorship and broad cross-functional exposure.
skills and experience required
Near/Full professional actuarial qualification with IFoA, SOA or equivalent.
Deep functional expertise across non-life pricing, reserving, capital modeling, and regulatory guidelines, with technical familiarity in tools such as ResQ, Igloo, Python, or R.
Extensive background operating within general insurance, reinsurance, or top-tier consulting environments.
Demonstrated ability to guide complex project deliverables, mentor junior team members, and manage end-to-end accountabilities.
Superior executive communication and stakeholder management capabilities to articulate complex technical concepts to non-technical business leaders.

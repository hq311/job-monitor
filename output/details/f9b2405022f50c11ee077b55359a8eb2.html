Job Purpose
The role sits in the Regional Life Actuarial team and will be responsible to support key regional projects of enhancing actuarial model, governance and reporting, and to collaborate with the local entities in achieving these goals.
What you do
1. Actuarial model and governance:
Support model enhancement, with a focus on asset-liability interaction and management rules
Perform model validation to ensure minimum requirements are met
Collaborate with local entities to enhance model documentation, including key methodologies, management actions, simplification list etc.
Coordinate model changes from local entities and ensure alignment with the Group model governance framework
2. Reporting:
Support reporting enhancement across entities, including movement analysis, reconciliations and driver attribution, in accordance to Group requirements and to support clearer explanation of IFRS17 and SII metrics
Improve reporting process, leveraging on automation
What you bring
More than 3+ years experience in insurance industry
Bachelor’s degree in Actuarial or equivalent
At least 3+ years of working experience in Actuarial function in dynamic insurance industry
Able to work independently
Work well in team and good interpersonal skill is essential
Willing to travel within Asia
Experience in Taiwan and in consulting
Good understanding of IFRS 17 Standards
Experience in financial analysis and Actuarial modelling
Proficiency in computer skills such as Microsoft Office suite, Visual Basic and PowerPoint
AI skills, e.g. ChatGPT and Copilot an advantage
Proven ability to multi-task and work under pressure
Able to speak other Asian language, e.g. Mandarin
Effective stakeholder management skills, with ability to communicate to a wide variety of levels

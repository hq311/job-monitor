Captured job description

At Income Insurance, we aim to create exponential value and cultivate dynamic learning culture. As part of Income Insurance Actuarial Team, candidate will work closely and collaborate with stakeholders across the organization to deliver business-oriented, customer-focused and innovative insurance solutions.
Responsibilities:
Support other team deliveries including User Acceptance Testing, business management reports, experience and profit monitoring and reinsurance tender.
Assist in the preparation of regulatory reporting filings
Collaborate and support business stakeholders to gather insights for purpose of delivering project assigned, using external and internal data sources
Adopt design-thinking and agile framework in delivering project
Ensure deliveries follows the department's best practice on risk and compliance
Requirements:
Bachelor’s Degree in Actuarial Science, Mathematics, Statistics or related field with good progress in actuarial credentials (e.g. SOA, IFoA, Institute of Actuaries Australia, or equivalent)
At least 1 year of relevant working experience, fresh graduates with relevant internship experience are welcome to apply.
Ability in critical thinking, problem solving and decision making
Excellent communication skills with the ability to present information clearly and concisely
Detail oriented with a commitment to accuracy and quality
Adaptable and open to dynamic working environment and willing to explore new ways of working
Strong teamwork and collaboration skills

About the company
A leading international life insurer with an established regional presence offers an exceptional platform focused on balance sheet optimization and sustained professional development.
About the job
This key role grants complete ownership over solvency frameworks and capital strategy, providing a strong platform to directly influence executive financial decisions.
Spearhead monthly and quarterly RBC2 capital adequacy calculations, regulatory submissions, and solvency ratio analysis.
Drive dynamic stress testing models and risk scenario evaluations to support the annual ORSA framework.
Optimize corporate balance sheet resilience by partnering with risk and investment divisions to model asset-liability matching strategies.
Elevate technical governance by serving as the subject matter expert on capital regulation updates and advising leadership committees.
About the manager/team
Reporting to the Head of Capital Management, you will operate within an agile, strategic unit that offers high visibility to C-suite decision-makers and broad cross-functional exposure.
Skills and experience required
Demonstrated expertise in life actuarial functions with hands-on experience in RBC2 capital reporting and solvency calculations.
Active progress toward professional actuarial designation such as FSA, FIA, FIAA, or an equivalent qualification.
Proficient in actuarial modeling platforms, specifically Prophet, alongside dynamic solvency stress testing methodologies.
Solid understanding of balance sheet management, capital optimization, and asset-liability matching principles.
Strong communication and stakeholder engagement skills to translate complex actuarial findings to business leadership.

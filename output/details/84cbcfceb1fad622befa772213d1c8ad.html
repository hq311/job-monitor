We are currently seeking a high calibre professional to join our team as a Senior Associate
In this role you will:
Support pricing review for Integrated Shield Plans, Individual Health and Employee Benefits products. Ensure compliance requirements are met and accurate system implementation.
Produce regular monitoring and profitability insights for senior stakeholders, including inflation studies and actual-vs-expected experience analyses; identify key profit/loss drivers and recommend actions.
Manage and curate data used for pricing, analytics, and performance monitoring to ensure accuracy, consistency, and auditability.
Own the Employee Benefits pricing tool, driving continuous enhancements to improve technical accuracy, usability, and processing efficiency.
Support Employee Benefits Large Quotation Review which exceeds underwriters' authority limit.
Provide financial assessments for various company initiatives such as campaigns.
To be successful you will need:
Independent learner with strong technical, analytical and problem-solving skills
Organized, detail-oriented, and motivated team player who is dynamic and adapts well to changes
Strong presentation, communication and interpersonal skills
Proficiency in Excel, VBA, Python

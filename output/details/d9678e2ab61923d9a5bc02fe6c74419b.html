We are currently seeking a high calibre professional to join our team as a Manager, Modeling (Actuarial)
In this role you will:
Perform actuarial model development and ensure compliance with applicable model risk policies and controls.
Provide modelling support and subject-matter expertise to actuarial teams.
Review and challenge model methodologies, assumptions, outputs and associated risks.
Drive efficiency improvements across actuarial models, tools and processes.
Support effective model governance, documentation, monitoring and issue remediation.
To be successful you will need:
Qualified / close-to-qualify actuary with membership of a recognised actuarial examination body.
Strong relevant actuarial experience, preferably including hands-on experience in actuarial modelling and stochastic valuation.
Knowledge in Prophet software is highly preferred.
Strong technical, analytical and problem-solving skills, with the ability to apply sound actuarial judgement.
Strong written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to explain complex actuarial concepts clearly to a range of stakeholders.

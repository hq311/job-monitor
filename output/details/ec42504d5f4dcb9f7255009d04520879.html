Job Profile Summary:
In Actuarial Pricing, the main responsibility is to price insurance products in accordance with internal financial benchmarks as well as regulatory requirements. The staff will be responsible in supporting the product delivery process, providing the actuarial requirements for product launches timely and accurately. The staff will also support all queries pertaining to product features and designs, as well as support any ad-hoc projects that may arise.
Job Description:
Understand and carry out the product pricing and delivery process.
Apply actuarial technical knowledge in making actual business decisions.
Learn principles of product pricing framework, regulatory requirements, and gain a sense of business acumen.
Support all queries pertaining to product features and designs.
Support any ad-hoc projects that may arise.
Competencies & Personal Traits
Good technical, analytical, and problem-solving skills, with the ability to work independently.
Self-motivated, outcome-oriented, and ability to deliver quality work under tight timelines.
Ability to communicate and collaborate with colleagues across different functions.
Some experience in any other type of Actuarial role is an advantage
Some experience with Prophet model, and Excel VBA is an advantage.
Professional Qualifications
Good progress in actuarial examinations from a recognized professional actuarial body.
Education
Degree in Actuarial Science or related disciplines (Mathematics, Statistics, Finance, Economics, etc.)

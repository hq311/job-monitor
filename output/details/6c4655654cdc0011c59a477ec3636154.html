About the company
A well-established global insurance company with a strong market presence is expanding its regional governance capabilities.
About the job
This is a unique opportunity for an actuarial specialist looking to transition into a strategic internal audit position with long-term leadership potential.
Plan, direct, and execute comprehensive audit programs across core business divisions.
Evaluate internal control effectiveness and deliver actionable risk-mitigation insights to business leaders.
Prepare clear audit findings and reports for presentation to executive stakeholders.
Collaborate across lines of defense to identify emerging risks and advise on key business initiatives.
Mentor junior team members while progressively building the capacity to step into an audit lead role.
Skills and experience required
At least 6 to 8 years of relevant actuarial experience, ideally in Life or Health insurance.
Solid understanding of risk management principles, internal control systems, or data analytics capabilities.
Strong strategic thinking, high attention to detail, and sound analytical judgment.
Excellent interpersonal and communication skills to effectively engage and collaborate with senior stakeholders.

Shape Your Future With Us
General Re Corporation, a subsidiary of Berkshire Hathaway Inc., is a holding company for global reinsurance and related operations, with more than 2,000 employees worldwide. It owns General Reinsurance Corporation and General Reinsurance AG, which conducts business as Gen Re.
Gen Re delivers reinsurance solutions to the Life/Health and Property/Casualty insurance industries. Represented in all major reinsurance markets through a network of 37 offices, we have earned superior financial strength ratings from each of the major rating agencies. We value diversity, equality and inclusion and promote a culture that supports professional and personal growth.
Your Role
Our Asia Life/Health unit is looking for an energetic and dynamic Marketing Actuary to grow our business in the ASEAN region, with a particular focus on Vietnam and other markets in Mainland Southeast Asia. You will serve as an account manager for your assigned clients and territories, managing the full deal cycle from identifying opportunities and developing proposals to negotiating and closing contracts. In this role, you will build trusted relationships with key client stakeholders, including Chief Financial Officers (CFOs), chief actuaries, heads of product, and underwriting managers.
Your Key Responsibilities
Plan and implement Gen Re's market strategy for your assigned territories
Monitor market trends, competitors, and regulatory developments to identify new business opportunities
Partner with clients on product development from concept to launch
Prepare reinsurance quotations and perform product pricing in line with our guidelines
Negotiate and draft reinsurance contracts
Monitor the performance of assigned in-force portfolio, including premiums, profitability, and claims experience
Build strong client relationships and strengthen Gen Re's market presence
Contribute to regional and international projects
Your Profile
Qualified or nearly qualified actuary
At least 5 years’ experience in life insurance or reinsurance company
Experience in or knowledge of the Vietnamese life insurance market is an advantage
Proficient in English and Vietnamese, both written and spoken. English is the primary working language, while Vietnamese is required for effective communication and relationship management with external stakeholders.
Willing and able to travel regularly for business
Excellent communication and interpersonal skills
Strong ability to work autonomously and collaboratively

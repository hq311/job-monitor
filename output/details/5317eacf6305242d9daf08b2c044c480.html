Captured job description

A regional life insurer in Singapore is looking for an Actuarial Pricing Manager to join their life pricing team, working closely with the Head of Pricing on new and repriced product launches.
This is a chance to take real ownership of pricing strategy at a well-established regional insurer, with visibility up to senior leadership and a clear roadmap to build hands-on exposure in the life insurance portfolio.
What you'll be doing:
Build actuarial pricing models for new and repriced life products, including financial assessment and sensitivity testing (i.e. HNW / Par / Non-Par / ILP)
Identify and manage pricing risks, partnering cross-functionally (product, IT, risk & compliance) for compliant, audit-ready launches
Work with the Appointed Actuary on assumptions, and support reinsurance discussions and negotiations
Guide junior pricing actuaries and drive quality across the pricing process
What you'll bring:
6-10 years of actuarial experience, with strong track record of life pricing experience in the Singapore market - This is essential
Actuarial modelling proficiency (e.g. Prophet, AXIS, RAFM, PathWise) is highly advantageous
Strong knowledge on RBC2, EV, and/or IFRS17

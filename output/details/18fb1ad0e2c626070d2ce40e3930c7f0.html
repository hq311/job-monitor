Roles and Responsibilities:
Actuarial - Valuation:
Responsible for the preparation of reserves, new business embedded value, statutory forms and other internal and external reports
Perform analysis of results, including explanation of movement in valuation results
Provide support in enhancing and modification of the valuation system
Monitor the financial positions of the company to ensure ongoing RBC2 solvency
Assist to manage valuation related projects and coordinate with internal and external parties
Involve in the annual review exercise of experience studies, bonus investigation, stress test and policies such as reserving SOP, NBEV SOP, CAR computation SOP and Par Internal Governance Policy
Assist in the preparation of annual SAA review and business plan
Others:
To participate in other projects assigned by the Chief Actuary
Job Requirements:
Good degree in Actuarial Science or related subjects
Job title and rank depend on the number of years actuarial working experience in direct life insurance business or consulting firms
Familiar with Singapore RBC2 regulations and RBC2 development is an advantage
Familiar with actuarial reserving and embedded value reporting
Proficient in MS Excel and MS Access
Good communication and project management skills
Ability to read Chinese will be an advantage

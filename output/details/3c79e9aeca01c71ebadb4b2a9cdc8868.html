We are currently seeking a high calibre professional to join our team as a ASSOCIATE, ACTUARIAL SERVICES
In this role you will:
The jobholder is part of Par & Universal Life Fund Management and Experience Studies team.
The jobholder is mainly responsible for supporting experience monitoring, assumption setting, impact/sensitivity testing, and Universal Life Fund Management in the Actuarial Services Department to support business needs.
Supporting the monthly and quarterly delivery of management information for internal stakeholders.
Supporting in ad-hoc actuarial projects and onboarding of new products.
Working closely with the group office and external Actuarial consultants, ensuring adherence to the HSBC guidelines and statutory regulations.
To be successful you will need:
0 to 2 years of Actuarial experience
Excellent communication skills, both written and spoken.
Ability to work independently, work well with others as interaction with other departments may be required.
Good technical, analytical and problem solving skills.
Team player with good interpersonal skills and positive attitude.
Experience in SQL/Python and/or Prophet is a plus.

About Our Client
Our client is a well-established player in the insurance industry with a strong presence across multiple markets. The organization is recognized for its commitment to innovation, robust risk management practices, and collaborative working environment. Employees have the opportunity to work alongside experienced actuarial professionals and contribute to projects that support business growth and strategic decision-making.
Job Description
As an Actuarial Analyst, you will support a range of actuarial activities across experience studies, fund management, and business initiatives. Key responsibilities include:
Assist in experience monitoring and analysis to support actuarial decision-making.
Support assumption setting exercises and sensitivity testing activities.
Contribute to the management and reporting of actuarial and business insights.
Prepare monthly and quarterly management information for key stakeholders.
Participate in product onboarding and implementation initiatives.
Provide actuarial support for ad hoc projects and business requirements.
Collaborate with regional teams, consultants, and internal stakeholders to ensure compliance with regulatory and governance requirements.
The Successful Applicant
You will ideally possess:
Min 2 years of actuarial experience, preferably within life insurance or financial services.
Bachelor's degree in Actuarial Science, Mathematics, Statistics, Quantitative Finance, Economics, Data Science, Engineering, or a related quantitative discipline.
Strong analytical and problem-solving capabilities.
Effective written and verbal communication skills.
Ability to work independently while also collaborating within cross-functional teams.
Strong interpersonal skills and a positive, proactive attitude.
Exposure to actuarial modelling or data analytics tools such as Prophet, SQL, or Python would be advantageous.
Progress toward actuarial professional qualifications is preferred but not essential.
What's on Offer
This opportunity offers valuable exposure to actuarial experience studies, fund management activities, and strategic business projects within a leading insurance environment. Successful candidates can expect a collaborative culture, opportunities to learn from experienced actuarial professionals, and exposure to regional stakeholders and business initiatives that will support long-term career development.

Captured job description

Our client, a leading life insurer, serves clients across Asia-Pacific and has a strong, well-capitalised book. They are seeking a high calibre professional to join the Financial Risk Management team as VP (Solvency).
The Role
You’ll own end-to-end Solvency II production and turn the numbers into insight that shapes capital, product and investment decisions.
Key responsibilities
Lead Solvency II production, including reconciliations, capital movement analysis, sensitivities and a controlled reporting timetable
Develop and govern the actuarial models, assumptions and methodologies behind Solvency II, including data validation, expert judgement and change controls
Assess the solvency impact of new products, investment and reinsurance decisions, and business planning, and present recommendations to management and committees
Design and interpret stress tests and scenario analysis, highlighting vulnerabilities and mitigating actions
Contribute to the ORSA and risk reporting, and support the independent review of the RBC2 Matching Adjustment
What you’ll bring
Qualified or near-qualified actuary, with 8+ years of experience in the life insurance industry
Strong experience in the following: Solvency II, regulatory and economic capital, stress testing and scenario analysis
Experience challenging model outputs and methodologies; exposure to internal model, standard formula or model governance is a plus
A track record of leading accurate reporting cycles through reconciliations, sign-offs, governance papers and regulatory submissions
The ability to explain complex results clearly and influence senior stakeholders across functions

Job Purpose
We are seeking a motivated and innovative Associate AI Engineer to join us. In this role, you will support the design, development, and deployment of AI and machine learning solutions that enhance customer experiences, improve business processes, and drive operational efficiency.
Working alongside experienced Data Scientists, AI Engineers, and business stakeholders, you will gain hands-on experience with machine learning models, generative AI applications, AI agents, and cloud-based AI platforms.
Key Responsibilities
Assist in developing and deploying AI and machine learning solutions to address business challenges and enhance operational performance.
Work with business and technical teams to understand requirements, gather data, and support the delivery of AI-driven use cases.
Build, test, and maintain machine learning models using structured and unstructured data.
Support the development of Generative AI applications, including chatbots, copilots, AI agents, document intelligence, and knowledge retrieval solutions.
Participate in the deployment and monitoring of AI solutions in cloud platforms such as Azure or AWS.
Assist in data preparation, feature engineering, model evaluation, and performance optimization activities.
Develop and maintain APIs and integrations to operationalize AI models and services.
Support AI model observability, monitoring, and reporting to ensure reliability and performance in production.
Collaborate with Data Engineers and Platform Engineers to improve AI and data infrastructure capabilities.
Stay current with emerging AI technologies, machine learning techniques, and industry best practices.
Education
Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Data Science, Artificial Intelligence, Engineering, Mathematics, Statistics, or a related discipline.
Technical Skills
1 to 3 years of experience in AI, machine learning, software engineering, data science, or related technical fields.
Basic understanding of machine learning concepts and algorithms.
Knowledge of Python and common data science libraries such as Pandas, NumPy, Scikit-learn, and PyTorch or TensorFlow.
Familiarity with Generative AI and Large Language Models (LLMs).
Understanding of prompt engineering, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), or AI Agents is advantageous.
Exposure to Natural Language Processing (NLP), OCR, computer vision, or document intelligence solutions is a plus.
Basic understanding of cloud platforms such as Microsoft Azure or AWS.
Familiarity with API development frameworks such as FastAPI or Flask is desirable.
Knowledge of Git, CI/CD practices, and MLOps concepts is beneficial.
Soft Skills
Strong analytical and problem-solving skills.
Curiosity and passion for learning new technologies.
Good communication and collaboration skills.
Ability to work effectively in a team environment.
Willingness to learn from senior engineers and contribute to innovative AI initiatives.
Preferred Experience
Academic, internship, or project experience involving machine learning, AI, software development, or data analytics.
Exposure to Generative AI technologies such as Azure OpenAI, OpenAI, Anthropic Claude, or similar platforms.
Experience in insurance, financial services, or regulated industries would be advantageous but not required.

Job Description & Summary
About PwC:
PwC is a global network of firms delivering world-class assurance, tax, and consulting services. We help resolve complex issues and identify opportunities across diverse industries. Our teams are committed to delivering high-quality services that help our clients build trust and create sustainable success.
Role Overview:
As an Actuarial Senior Associate/Manager at PwC, you will be a key member of our Financial Services team. You will lead and support engagements across a wide range of actuarial functions, providing expert insights and solutions to our clients. You will collaborate with cross-functional teams, manage client relationships, and contribute to the growth and development of our actuarial practice.
Key Responsibilities:
Lead and manage actuarial projects for our insurance clients.
Conduct actuarial analysis and modelling, including risk assessment, pricing, reserving, and financial projections.
Develop and maintain strong client relationships, understanding their needs and delivering tailored solutions.
Provide technical expertise and guidance to junior team members, fostering their professional growth.
Collaborate with other PwC teams to deliver integrated solutions and enhance client outcomes.
Stay updated on industry trends, regulatory changes, and emerging technologies impacting the actuarial field.
Contribute to business development efforts, including proposal writing and client presentations.
Qualifications:
Bachelor's degree in Actuarial Science, Mathematics, Statistics, or a related field; advanced degree preferred.
Working towards professional actuarial designation.
2-3 years of experience in actuarial roles, with a focus on life insurance. Less experienced candidate may be considered for a junior role.
Strong analytical skills and proficiency in actuarial software and tools (e.g., Prophet, Pathwise, Excel, R, Python, SAS).
Excellent communication and interpersonal skills, with the ability to present complex information clearly.
Proven project management skills and the ability to lead and mentor teams.
Strong business acumen and problem-solving capabilities.
Why PwC?
Access to a diverse range of clients and projects that will challenge and expand your expertise.
Opportunities for professional development and career advancement.
A collaborative and inclusive work environment that values innovation and diversity.
Competitive compensation and benefits package.
